Taxes and withdrawal order

The optimizer rescans the order each projection year using age, early-withdrawal penalties, taxable basis, tax-deferred concentration, RMD timing, IRMAA headroom, survivor and estate goals, recent market returns, asset risk, cash reserves, debt costs, and spending flexibility. It is a planning heuristic, not individualized tax advice.

Asset classes, correlated returns, and rebalancing Advanced
Correlation is unitless and estimates how differently asset classes move. +1 means they move together, 0 means no consistent relationship, and -1 means they move in opposite directions. A lower value generally produces more diversification and lower modeled portfolio volatility.

Monte Carlo and historical testing Advanced

Red stress years and green favorable years are research-informed test presets, not predictions. Both groups remain visible in this shared Monte Carlo and historical-testing section; the selected year controls historical replay. Historical replay always uses matching inflation and available historical Social Security COLAs.

Monte Carlo varies the order and size of annual returns to show sequence-of-returns risk and the share of simulated paths that fully fund requested spending. A seed makes the random paths repeatable: the same inputs and seed produce the same result. The 10,000-run ceiling is intended to remain practical on a flagship phone.
