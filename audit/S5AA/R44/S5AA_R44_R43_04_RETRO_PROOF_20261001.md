# S5AA R44 — R43-04: the corrected scans, proved on R43's pre-repair trees

*Written by Claude on 2026-10-01 (Arizona, UTC−7), on the owner's decision of that day: R43-04 (five prediction misses) is
not accepted as a disclosed miss. It requires a written checklist (`S5AA_R44_PREDICTION_CHECKLIST_20261001.md`) and a proof,
on R43's own trees, that the corrected scans would have predicted what actually moved. The scripts and raw outputs are in
`prediction/retro/`.*

## What was done

1. **Measured the truth** (`measured_movement.txt`). For each R43 part with a miss, the expanded corpus (71 plans, the
   control's 36 included) was captured before and after the part, each in a clean worktree, and compared entry by entry.
   This is the actual movement, independent of the records:

   | part | before → after | moved |
   |---|---|---|
   | 2, tax | `d11017f` → `b131aeb` | `expansion:monte-carlo-sensitive-band` |
   | 3, contributions | `b131aeb` → `dd18f31` | `seed:4` |
   | 4a, life events | `dd18f31` → `960eb11` | `expansion:s5aa-r6-gap-survivor-health-roth` |
   | 4b, cash flows | `960eb11` → `ae93a43` | 15 plans: `golden:reserve-and-bond-tent`, `seed:1` to `seed:7`, `seed:9`, `seed:11`, `seed:12`, `seed:15`, `seed:16`, `seed:19`, `targeted:spouse-cola-income` |

   It agrees with each part's commit record.
2. **Built the corrected scans from the committed R43 scans** (`make_v2_scans.js`), so each `_v2` file differs from its
   R43 original by its corrections alone. Each correction names its checklist item.
3. **Ran each corrected scan on its part's pre-repair tree,** in a clean worktree (`run_retro.sh`; outputs
   `*_v2_at_<commit>.txt`). Then compared the plans it names with the measured truth (`compare_retro.js`).

### Each correction

- **Part 2, SA42F-08 (C2):** a row counts only while the owner's IRA window is open (`ownerContributionWindow()`).
  `seed:3` and `seed:17` drop out: every row the R43 scan flagged was past their contribution stop.
- **Part 2, SA42F-22 (C6, C4): SA43-B's cause was not what R43 recorded.**
  - R43's self-audit said a band path's IRMAA charge moved. The band member has health costs **off**, so no IRMAA is
    ever charged on it.
  - What moved is the **optimized withdrawal order's IRMAA guard.** `smartWithdrawalOrder()` adds weight to the pre-tax
    class when someone is 63 or over and last year's MAGI is within the larger of $10,000 and 35% of spending of the
    next threshold, and it reads the same joint table.
  - The corrected scan lists both readers. For the guard, on each path, it asks the engine's own `smartWithdrawalOrder()`
    whether the order changes between the old and new tables, using that path's opening balances and MAGI history.
  - **It names the band member,** flagging 10 paths: all 6 that changed (125, 211, 219, 349, 377, 389) and 4 that did
    not.
  - **It also names `golden:monte-carlo-fixed-seed`.** Measured directly (`path_level_check.js`), four of its 500 paths
    do change (89, 174, 207, 390; the scan found three), but its median rows and success rate are identical. The change
    never reaches a reported figure.
  - This is why C4 predicts a Monte Carlo plan as "paths change; the published result may move". By that rule, both
    plans are stochastic predictions: one moved and one did not, and both outcomes are consistent with the prediction.
    By the plan-level rule alone, part 2 still has one over-prediction.
- **Part 3, SA43-C (C5):**
  - The direction now comes from the pre-repair engine run on the same plan, with each HSA's contribution set to 0 from
    its owner's 65 (an input that deposits nothing and redirects nothing after 65, as the ruling does).
  - It gives: taxes fall, the taxable balance falls, the HSA balance rises, contributions fall. That is what happened.
  - At that tree a change dated inside a row starts at the next row, where the ruling prorates. So the sizes differ
    slightly from the build's, but not the directions.
- **Part 4a, SA43-D (C1):** "alive" is the engine's `householdSeniorAges()`. The spouse, whose lifespan equals their age
  at the row's opening, counts as alive in that row, and the scan names the plan.
- **Part 4b, SA43-E (C3):** a stream counts only if the engine's `otherIncomeFor()`, given that stream alone, pays it in
  some row. `seed:8`'s zero-length stream drops out.

Every corrected scan still passes its R43 positive controls, witness and control alike (in each output's last section).

## What this proves, and what it does not

- **It proves:** on R43's own pre-repair trees, the corrected method names every plan that moved and none that did not
  in parts 3, 4a and 4b, with seed:4's direction right. In part 2 it catches the miss and drops the over-prediction,
  leaving one Monte Carlo plan that C4 now classes as a stochastic prediction.
- **It does not prove** the method catches misses no one has seen yet. The corrections were designed knowing these five
  misses, so this proof shows they are fixed, not that the next scan will be right. R44's own prediction, written under
  the checklist before its edits, is the test of that, and its comparison will say how it went.
- **SA43-B's cause is corrected here.** R43's self-audit and handover said the band member moved through a per-path
  IRMAA charge. It moved through the withdrawal order's IRMAA guard. The committed R43 records are left as they are;
  this record is the correction.
